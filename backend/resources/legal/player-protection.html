We follow the government's Voluntary Code of Good Practice for Prize Draw Operators. This page sets out the measures we have in place for each part of the Code.

Age

Our competitions are for people aged 18 or over who live in the UK. You give your date of birth when you join, and we check identity before we pay a prize. We never aim our advertising at under-18s.

Credit Cards

You can't pay for instant win competitions with a credit card. For live draws, we accept no more than £250 a month in credit card payments from any player. Wallet top-ups and memberships are paid by debit card. When only a debit card can be used, we tell you before you pay.

Spending Limits

You can set daily, weekly and monthly limits on what you pay in, including a limit of £0. We offer you a monthly limit when you join, and again before your second entry. Lowering a limit applies straight away. Raising or removing one takes effect after 24 hours.

Breaks, Suspension And Closing Your Account

You can take a short break (from a day to six weeks) or self-exclude for six months or longer. Both apply straight away. While they last, you can't enter and we send you no marketing. You can also ask us to close your account at any time.

Looking Out For Harm

From the day you join, we watch for signs that play may be causing harm. These include:

  • reaching or raising a limit;
  • high spending;
  • frequent top-ups;
  • using several different cards;
  • returning straight after a break.

A member of our team reviews each one. They take a step that fits what they find, which can include contacting you or pausing your account.

Support

If you're worried about your play or money, free, confidential help is available from:

  • Citizens Advice
  • National Debtline
  • Samaritans (116 123)
  • Mind

Our Safer Play page has your limits and breaks.

How Our Draws Work

Every competition page sets out how it runs: the price, the maximum number of tickets, when it closes, and when and how the winner is drawn. Prizes are awarded in accordance with the laws of chance.

Winners are chosen by our draw engine from a public random number (the drand beacon), which no one can predict or change. Every draw has a record that anyone can check. The record shows every entry, the random number, the winning ticket and how one leads to the other.

Instant win results are fixed before the competition opens, and the winning ticket numbers are published.

Every entry, paid or free, has the same chance of winning each prize. Instant win competitions are never the majority of our competitions.

Free Entry

Every competition can be entered free by post, by ordinary first- or second-class post, with exactly the same chance of winning as a paid entry. The details are on every competition and on our Free Entry page.

Prizes

We award the advertised prize, or a cash alternative where one is offered. We never reduce a prize, move a closing date or cancel a draw because of low ticket sales.

Complaints

Our Complaints page explains how to complain and what happens next.

Marketing

Our advertising follows the CAP and BCAP codes. We never suggest that a competition is a way out of problems, a solution to money worries, an alternative to work or a route to financial security.
